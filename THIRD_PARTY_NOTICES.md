# Third-party components

The Tiangong Pro kinematics and collision envelopes are derived from the simulator's robot description. The original file and revision are identified in `upper_body.urdf`. The robot surface data also records its source and checksum. The upstream simulator repository includes its Apache 2.0 license.

[Upstream simulator and license](https://github.com/nssr-hackathon/nssr-uwt-polygon)
